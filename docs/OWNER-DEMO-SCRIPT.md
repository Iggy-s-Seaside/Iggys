# Iggy's Operating Platform — Live Demo Script

*A 5-minute walkthrough on Bradley's phone. One thumb, no laptop, no slides — just the real app.*

*Goal: by minute five the owner has felt the cockpit, watched the AI think, and seen a customer get an instant quote. Every beat below is a real screen in the app today.*

---

## Before You Walk In (60-second setup, do this in the car)

- [ ] Phone on Wi-Fi or strong signal; screen brightness up; **portrait lock on** (no surprise rotations).
- [ ] App open and **logged in** to the manager dashboard. Confirm the URL/PWA loads to the Dashboard, not the login screen.
- [ ] Confirm **Today's Pulse** shows real weather (it pulls live — glance that the temp looks right for Seaside today).
- [ ] Confirm there's **at least one fresh Luna insight** on the Dashboard feed with an Approve action. If the feed is thin, that's the only thing to stage beforehand.
- [ ] Open a **second tab / second phone** to the **public** site's Book-an-Event page, pre-loaded, so the estimator beat is instant.
- [ ] Hand-warm the phone, hand it to no one — **you drive the whole demo.** Owner watches over your shoulder. That framing ("I run this from right here") is half the pitch.

**One rule:** if anything is slow to load, keep talking — never narrate a spinner.

---

## The Click Path at a Glance

`Dashboard (Today's Pulse)` → `Luna insight → one-tap Approve` → `tonight's run sheet` → `revenue / reports view` → `flip to the public site → live estimator`

Five screens. Five moments. Five minutes.

---

## Beat 1 — Today's Pulse (0:00–0:45)

**Do:** Open the app. Land on the **Dashboard**. Don't scroll yet — let the **Today's Pulse** card sit there.

**Point at:** the one-line state of the bar and the **live weather/tide** in the card.

**Say:**
> "This is the first thing I see when I walk in. One line — the whole state of the bar tonight. And see the weather and the tide right there? That's pulled live for Seaside. No other bar tool does that, because none of them care that we're a coast town. The weather *is* our business."

**Why it lands:** five seconds in, the owner already sees something Toast and Square don't have. Set the hook: *this screen knows things.*

---

## Beat 2 — Luna Thinks Overnight, You Tap Once (0:45–2:00)

**Do:** Scroll a thumb's-length to the **Luna insights** feed on the Dashboard. Stop on the briefing/suggestion card that has an **Approve** button.

**Point at:** the insight title and body first, then the **Approve** chip.

**Say (read the actual insight out loud — it's more convincing than any pitch):**
> "Here's the part I love. Overnight — while we're closed — Luna reads the whole bar and writes me this. Listen: *[read the card]* — she's looked at the book, the inventory, and the weather, and she's telling me the one thing that needs my call today. And look — she's already **drafted the follow-up email.** I read it…"

**Do:** Tap **Approve** / **Review & send.** Show the drafted text pre-filled.

**Say:**
> "…and I send it under my own login. Here's the important part for you: **Luna never sends anything herself.** She drafts, I trigger. Every email, every charge, every post — a human pushes the button. She does the thinking and the typing; I stay in control. That's the AI everyone's selling — except we already own ours, so it's built in, not a $300-a-month add-on."

**The weather kicker (say it while the draft is on screen):**
> "And she's smart about Seaside specifically. A slow rainy Tuesday? She knows that's the weather and stays quiet. An open *sunny* night with no event? *That* she flags — because that one we can actually fill."

**Why it lands:** this is the emotional center of the whole meeting. The owner sees an AI that did real work overnight and handed it over as a one-tap decision, with the human firmly in charge.

---

## Beat 3 — Tonight's Run Sheet (2:00–2:45)

**Do:** From the Dashboard, open **tonight's run sheet / huddle** view (the auto-built "everything happening tonight" screen).

**Point at:** events, parties, active specials, and the low-stock line — all on one screen.

**Say:**
> "Before the shift, this builds itself. Every event, every private party, the specials that are live, and what we're low on — one screen. I used to keep this in my head and a group text. Now I read three bullets to the staff and we're set. Nobody finds out about the 40-top at 6:55."

**Why it lands:** it's the daily-grind relief. The owner pictures fewer fire drills and a manager who's never caught flat-footed.

---

## Beat 4 — The Money View (2:45–3:45)

**Do:** Navigate to the **revenue / reports** view — the parties revenue dashboard or the money strip (what's booked, what's owed, what's paid).

**Point at:** booked revenue, the owed-vs-paid chips, and the forward book.

**Say:**
> "Here's what you actually want to see. This is the private-events money — what's booked, what's still owed, what's already in. And this number here is the forward book: dollars confirmed for the next 90 days. No POS needed — this is real today."

**The Owner-Pack line (say it here, it's the recurring hook):**
> "And this same data composes itself into a one-page report — your branded KPIs with a written summary — that lands in your inbox **every Monday at 8am without anyone asking.** You get a finance department's worth of reporting that just shows up."

**The Stripe tease (don't fumble for a live charge unless it's staged — tease it instead):**
> "When we flip on Stripe, you'll watch a real card get charged right here on this phone. One rail — and it powers merch, deposits, gift cards, and tickets all at once. That's three of your four asks closing on one piece of plumbing."

**Why it lands:** this is the owner's language — revenue, owed, forward book — and the "it just shows up Monday" line is the one they'll repeat to their partner.

---

## Beat 5 — The Customer's Side: Instant Estimate (3:45–4:45)

**Do:** Flip to the **public** site (second tab / second phone), the **Book-an-Event** page. Scroll to the **packages** and the **estimator**.

**Do live, slowly, so they watch the number move:** pick a package → type a **guest count** (say "30") → set **hours** → let the **total appear instantly.**

**Say:**
> "Now here's what your *customer* sees. They pick a package, type in 30 guests and a couple hours — and watch — there's the **exact price, instantly.** Same math we quote by hand, no waiting on us, no phone tag. It qualifies the lead before they ever call."

**Do:** Point at the **Submit** button (don't submit unless staged).

**Say:**
> "And when they hit submit, that inquiry doesn't land in an inbox to get lost. It drops straight into our pipeline **already half-quoted** — package picked, guests in, contact saved. And Luna sees it the next morning and makes sure we follow up. That's a booking that used to take three calls, now starting itself."

**Why it lands:** it closes the loop the owner opened — "list our packages online" — and shows it's not just *listed*, it's *working*: instant quote in, qualified lead out.

---

## Beat 6 — The Close (4:45–5:00)

**Do:** Flip back to the **Dashboard / Today's Pulse**. Let it rest on screen — end where you started.

**Say:**
> "So — one screen runs the whole bar. An AI thinks about it overnight and hands me one-tap decisions. The money side reports itself. And a customer can get an instant quote and start a booking without us touching it. Every piece of this is 'AI that thinks about your bar' — and we already own the agent. The other guys charge hundreds a month for slices of it.
>
> I've got a short list of yes/no calls — Stripe, the deposit rule, the social paperwork. Give me those today, and this Pulse you're looking at becomes the way Iggy's runs."

**Then stop talking.** Let the screen and the silence do the closing.

---

## If Something Breaks (recovery lines, memorize these)

- **Weather/Pulse didn't load:** *"That pulls live — on a good signal it's instant. The point is it's in here, working for us every shift."* Move on.
- **No fresh Luna insight:** open the Luna chat instead and ask a real question out loud ("Luna, how did last Friday compare to a normal Friday?") — the grounded, sourced answer sells the same point.
- **Estimator number lags:** keep narrating the customer story; the number catches up. Never say "loading."
- **App is slow anywhere:** *"This is the dev build on bar Wi-Fi — production is snappier."* Keep moving; never stop on a spinner.

## The Three Lines That Must Land (if you forget everything else)

1. **"Luna drafts, I trigger — a human pushes every button."** *(trust)*
2. **"It shows up in your inbox Monday at 8am without anyone asking."** *(the recurring wow)*
3. **"We already own the AI — competitors charge hundreds a month for slices of this."** *(the moat)*
